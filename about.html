BEFORE YOU BOOK

Frequently Asked Questions

Confirm the latest details directly with Pari PG management.

How can I check room availability?

Visit the Rooms page and contact management to confirm which room types are available for your preferred move-in date.

Are the prices shown final?

The displayed room prices are demo values. Ask for the current rent, security deposit, included services and any additional charges.

Are meals and drinking water included?

Meal plans and drinking-water arrangements must be confirmed with management before booking.

Can I visit the PG before booking?

Contact management to ask about a visit and verify the actual room, facilities, location and house rules.

What should I bring when moving in?

After confirming the booking terms, ask management which documents and personal items are required.

How do I ask a question quickly?

Use the Contact page or the floating WhatsApp button once the official WhatsApp number has been configured.
